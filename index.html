Why I built it

Made for real parenting, not a spreadsheet

I am Tom, a dad of two in Ebbsfleet. When my youngest was a newborn and constantly poorly, my wife and I were forever trying to remember her last feed and scheduled medicine times.

Every app we tried wanted a monthly subscription for something this basic, or took six taps to log a single bottle. So I built the one we actually needed.

My wife tested every version before it went near the App Store, and most of what is good about it came from her using it for real.
